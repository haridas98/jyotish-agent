# Vaishnava Interpretation Policy

## Principle

All recommendations must be Krishna-centered and compatible with Srila Prabhupada's teachings and ISKCON parampara.

## Remedies

If a classical jyotish source recommends propitiating a graha or demigod, the public recommendation must be reframed as dependent devotional practice:

- chant the Hare Krishna maha-mantra;
- take shelter of Krishna;
- read Bhagavad-gita and Srimad-Bhagavatam;
- serve Vaishnavas;
- offer charity, vrata, or worship only as connected to Krishna and guru-parampara;
- avoid presenting any demigod as independent from Krishna.

## Prohibited Output

- "Worship this demigod independently to solve the problem."
- "A planet forces this result with certainty."
- "This chart proves spiritual advancement or failure."
- Medical, legal, or financial directives.

## Required Tone

Predictions should be probabilistic, humble, and tied to free will, karma, and devotional service.
